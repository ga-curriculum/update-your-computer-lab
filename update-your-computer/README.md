**Learning objective:** By the end of this lesson, students will be able to check their current OS version, determine if updates are available, and apply those updates to their system.

# Checking Your OS Version and Update Status

Identify your current OS version and check for available updates.

## For Windows

- Click on the Start menu and select "Settings" (gear icon).

- Click on "System" and then "About."

- Under "Windows specifications," find the "Version" information.

- Return to the main "Settings" menu and click on "Update & Security."

- Click on "Windows Update" and then "Check for updates."

## For macOS

- Click on the Apple menu in the top-left corner of the screen.

- Select "About This Mac."

- The current macOS version will be displayed in the "Overview" tab.

- Click on "Software Update" to check for available updates.

## Reflection

- What is your current OS version?
- Are there any updates available for your system?

# Applying the Latest Updates to Your OS

Perform an OS update following the standard procedures.

## For Windows

- In the "Windows Update" settings, click on "Download and install" if updates are available.

- Wait for the updates to download and install. Your computer may restart several times during this process.

- If prompted, click "Restart now" to complete the update process.

## For macOS

- In the "Software Update" window, click "Update Now" if updates are available.

- Wait for the updates to download and install. Your Mac may restart during this process.

- If prompted, enter your admin password to allow the installation to proceed.

## Reflection

- How long did the update process take?
- Did you encounter any issues or prompts during the update?

# Verifying the Update and Reviewing Changes

Confirm that the update was successful and explore any new features or changes.

## For Windows

- After your computer restarts, return to the "Settings" menu and navigate to "Update & Security."

- Click on "View update history" to confirm that the update was successfully installed.

- Explore any new features or changes by clicking on "Learn more" or visiting Microsoft's "What's new in Windows" webpage.

## For macOS

- After your Mac restarts, click on the Apple menu and select "About This Mac."

- Verify that the OS version has changed to the latest update.

- Explore any new features or changes by clicking on the "What's New" button or visiting Apple's "What's new in macOS" webpage.

## Reflection

- What new features or improvements did you discover after updating your OS?
- How might these updates enhance your user experience or system security?
